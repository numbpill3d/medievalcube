Character Chronicle

Born in the shadow of Castle Ravenspire, Silas Blackwood was the third son of a humble blacksmith. While his brothers followed their father's trade, Silas found himself drawn to the stories of knights and their noble quests.

At the age of sixteen, he left his village to serve as a squire to Sir Edric of Oakenshield, a renowned knight of the realm. For seven years, Silas learned the arts of combat, chivalry, and survival from his mentor.

His opportunity for knighthood came during the Battle of Grimhold Pass, where he saved Sir Edric's life and helped secure victory against the invading horde. Knighted on the battlefield by the king himself, Sir Silas has since wandered the realm, offering his blade to those in need.

Now in his thirtieth year, Sir Silas has earned a reputation as a steadfast defender of the innocent and a relentless hunter of dark creatures that plague the borderlands.
